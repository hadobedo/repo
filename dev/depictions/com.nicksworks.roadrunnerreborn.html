RoadRunner Reborn

Version 1.1.2

Nick's Works · iOS 15+

About

RoadRunner Reborn makes your Now Playing app (and optionally other selected apps) stay alive through sbreload and resprings, keeping your music and other apps uninterrupted!

Tested on iOS 15 - 17 rootless, should work on rootHide and iOS 18 and newer (fingers crossed)

Changelog

1.1.2

  • Apps that are meant to survive are captured more reliably.
  • Survivor handoff data is now stored properly in a plist instead of the temp directory.
  • Invalid survivor records are ignored instead of being restored.
  • SpringBoard and runningboardd coordinate so they do not overwrite each other’s changes.
  • Blacklist mode keeps its existing app list when iOS temporarily cannot provide a valid app list.
  • Missing process information no longer causes SpringBoard to crash randomly.
  • The system now-playing session is restored after resprings, so Dynamic Peninsula and lock screen cards recover without restarting the app.
  • Avoids sending a premature SpringBoard now-playing refresh before MediaRemote has a valid player path.
  • Removes the final MediaRemote override cleanup call to avoid launch-time notification churn.
  • Filters incomplete Dynamic Peninsula player paths so valid MediaRemote metadata can arrive cleanly after a respring.
  • Installs the Dynamic Peninsula compatibility filter when its image loads, instead of only during SpringBoard startup.
  • Requests a Dynamic Peninsula-owned now-playing refresh after RoadRunner reattaches the surviving media app.
  • Seeds Dynamic Peninsula with MediaRemote's validated local player path before requesting metadata.
